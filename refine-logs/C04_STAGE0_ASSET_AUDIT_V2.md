## Binding finding

No existing HateMM+MHC-ZH bank instantiates the four proposed variables
source/proposition/presenter-stance/protected-target-harm. P8 summaries retain
target/offensiveness language, K4 is only evidence density, and deterministic
lexical source/stance cues are not teacher-extracted roles. Those assets may be
reported as nonbinding diagnostics only. A positive proxy result cannot pass
C04; a negative proxy result cannot kill it.

The user-approved matched-signal route is therefore exactly the serial route in
`C04_USER_AMENDMENT_V2.md`: label-blind 200+200 train IDs, seal, small gate,
fresh independent GO, then at most the remaining train bank. No other extraction
route is legal.

## Data boundaries

Allowed before the teacher cache seal:

- the selected train ID;
- eight deterministic full-video frames;
- native ASR transcript, NFKC-normalized and capped to 2,048 Unicode scalar
  values as first 1,024 + literal separator + last 1,024;
- fixed policy-free extraction schema and prompt.

Forbidden:

- any label field or label-bearing container value;
- dev/test path, content, metadata, cache or teacher output;
- OCR, title, archive, prior teacher summary, prediction, neighbor, error list,
  score, rank, margin, baseline output or split statistic;
- external API/network inference, cross-dataset information or resampling.

Train labels become available only after the two-prompt cache, ID coverage,
access ledger, source/config hashes and Merkle root are sealed. Labels may then
be used only for deterministic folds, ordinary losses and evaluation.

conditional completion has a hard aggregate `<=8 GPU-hour` ledger including the
small tranche and every later C04 GPU allocation. There is no automatic full
