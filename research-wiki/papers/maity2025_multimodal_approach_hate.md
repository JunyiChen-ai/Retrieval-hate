# A Multimodal Approach for Hate and Offensive Content Detection in Tamil: From Corpus Creation to Model Development

## One-line thesis
Introduces the MultimodAl Tamil Hate corpus (Tamil videos with audio + transcripts annotated into offensive/sexist/racist/casteist categories) and shows that fusing transformer-based video, audio, and text features detects hate/offensive content, with text being the dominant signal.
