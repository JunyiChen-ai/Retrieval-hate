# Detecting hate in diversity: a survey of multilingual code-mixed image and video analysis

## One-line thesis
A literature survey arguing that hate-speech detection has over-focused on English unimodal text and should expand to code-mixed, multilingual, and multimodal (text/audio/image/video) settings; catalogs methods/datasets and proposes (conceptually) a CLIP + multilingual-BERT/XLM-R fusion framework. Video/audio hate is flagged but not deeply treated. ADJACENT field-map only, NOT a video method.
