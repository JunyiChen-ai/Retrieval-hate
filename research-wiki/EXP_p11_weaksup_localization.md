## 0. Campaign position (honest multiplicity accounting)

P11 is the **13th pre-registered route** in the MLLM-method-role campaign
(`CAMPAIGN_mllm_method_role.md`, `novelty-scope-and-plan.md`): after P1, P2, P2b/P2c,
P3 (EN/ZH/HateMM), P4, P5, P6, P7, P8/P8b, P9/P9b, P10/P10-b/P10-c. Of the prior
routes the MLLM earned exactly **two removable method roles — encoder + span-free
localization *scorer* (P6)** — plus guard-rail/audit; every **main-table-accuracy**
route was refuted, and the localization *scorer* was shown **modest** (P6 HateClipSeg
within-video AUC 0.5435; P10-b 72B A-fuse amplified it to 0.5755, still < the 0.60
"substantial" bar; P10-c open-generation jump FAILed calibration-side).

**What is still unopened (the reason P11 exists):** every localization result so far
uses the MLLM density scores as a **zero-training scorer** (rank windows directly).
The one in-discipline lever never tried is to use those scores as a **training
signal** — distil the MLLM's per-segment density into a **trained segment head** and
ask whether a learned head beats (a) a video-label-only MIL head and (c) a
non-MLLM (memory-kNN) weak-labeller, on the empty HateClipSeg weakly-supervised
localization track. This is the "span-free MLLM-weak-supervised localization" route.

**Red lines respected.** MultiHateLoc (WWW 2026, weakly-supervised MIL Top-K) has **no
code → NOT reimplemented**; our own simple top-k MIL head (arm A) is our own
implementation and is allowed. **NO ensembles** (single-model heads only). GPU only
via SLURM (no `--time`). MLLM scores are the already-landed P6/P10 assets (no new
scoring model is introduced).

## 1. Task & data

**Task:** HateClipSeg **segment-level hate localization** — score every temporal
segment of a video for hate; evaluated as within-video ranking (segment/second
binary AUC), the same frozen P6 harness (`p6_eval_localization.py` →
`eval_localization_hateclipseg.py`), gold segments = validation only, no HateClipSeg
label enters any scoring/training path except as the eval target.

**Segment granularity:** K=30 / M=120 windows (window median ≈ 8s, density-matched to
the 8.12s gold-segment median; the granularity at which P6/P10 report). Per-window
frozen-CLIP ViT-L/14-336 features (existing cache
`data/CLIP_Embedding/HateClipSeg/test_seen_subclipK30_*.pt`, [395, 30, 1024]).

**Split (DECLARED HERE, frozen this commit):** HateClipSeg has no official split; we
declare our own **60 / 10 / 30 by video** (train / val / test), seed 0, **stratified**
by whether the video contains ≥1 toxic gold second, generated by
`scripts/analysis/p11_make_split.py` → `data/gt/HateClipSeg/p11_split.json` (committed).
Approx 237 / 39 / 119 videos. **The test split is touched exactly once**, at the very
end, for the promoted configuration only.

> **Zero-shot-eval-vs-training-contamination declaration.** The HateClipSeg test
> videos were previously **zero-shot evaluated** (P6 wv-AUC 0.5435; P10-b 0.5755) —
> i.e. their windows were *scored*, never *trained on*, and no gold label ever entered
> those scores. Zero-shot evaluation is **not** training contamination: no P11 head
> ever sees a gold segment label, and the arm-B/C soft labels (MLLM / memory scores)
> are themselves gold-free. We nonetheless declare it explicitly: the same 395 videos
> were previously scored zero-shot; P11 additionally *trains* a head on the
> **train-split** videos' gold-free soft labels and reports the **test-split** once.

## 2. Three arms (same segment features, same head, ONLY the supervision differs)

All three arms share **identical** inputs and head:
- **Input:** per-window frozen-CLIP feature `x_{v,k}` ∈ R^1024 (K=30), L2-normalised.
- **Head:** a small MLP `f: R^1024 → R` (1024→256→1, ReLU, dropout 0.1) giving a
  per-segment logit `s_{v,k}=f(x_{v,k})`; **shared architecture across arms**. Segment
  score at eval = `s_{v,k}` (monotone; sigmoid optional, AUC-invariant).

Only the **training target** differs:

| arm | supervision signal | loss |
|---|---|---|
| **A — MIL baseline** (our own impl) | video-level label only | top-k MIL: video logit = mean of the top-k segment logits (k selected on val ∈{1,3,5}); BCE vs the video label. **No per-segment target.** |
| **B — MLLM weak-sup (ours)** | 72B A-fuse per-segment density 0–3 (P10-b asset), normalised to [0,1] as a **soft per-segment target** | per-segment soft-label distillation (MSE to the normalised density) + a video-label top-k MIL auxiliary (same weight schedule as A, so B and A share the label term; B **adds** the per-segment MLLM target). 7B K30 density = ablation labeller. |
| **C — memory-kNN weak-sup (control)** | consensus-kNN memory vote per segment (HateMM-subclip memory, the P6 memory row) as the **soft per-segment target** | **identical loss form to B**, only the labeller swapped (memory instead of MLLM) → isolates "MLLM vs non-MLLM weak-labeller". |

B vs C share the loss form and differ **only** in the weak-labeller → the B−C delta is
a clean "does the MLLM weak label beat a memory weak label" test. B vs A is "does any
per-segment weak label beat video-label-only MIL".

Selection: per-arm best epoch/hyperparam by **val-split** within-video AUC only
(val touched freely; test once). Report ≥3 seeds; paired per-video deltas on test.

## 3. Pre-registered success line (delivers the "substantial" semantics)

On the **test split**, ALL of:
1. **B − A** paired within-video AUC ≥ **+0.05**, bootstrap 95% CI(Δ) excludes 0.
2. **B − C** paired within-video AUC ≥ **+0.05**, bootstrap 95% CI(Δ) excludes 0.
3. **B absolute** within-video AUC ≥ **0.65**.

Not met on any of the three ⇒ **honest FAIL** (route closed, reported with mechanism).

> **Why 0.65 (training protocol) not 0.60 (zero-shot).** The zero-shot "substantial"
> line was 0.60 (P6/P10) — that bar priced in *no training*. A *trained* head that
> merely reproduces the zero-shot scorer (teacher 0.5755) delivers nothing new; a
> training route only earns its name if learning a head from the weak labels lifts the
> localizer **beyond** the zero-shot teacher by a clear margin. 0.65 = teacher 0.5755
> + ≈ +0.075 = a genuine training-borne gain. **Acknowledged as demanding:** a student
> distilled from a soft label rarely exceeds its teacher's own signal by +0.075 (the
> head can only denoise via feature smoothness), so the *training* bar is where this
> route most plausibly dies even if the probe passes — which is exactly why the cheap
> probe gate (§4) runs first and can kill before any GPU training is spent.

## 4. Probe gate — CHEAP, runs BEFORE any training, can kill P11 outright

**Question:** do the MLLM weak labels carry gold-span-localization signal that a
video-label MIL proxy does not? If not, arm B cannot beat arm A and training is
pointless.

**Where:** the **HateMM calibration set** (has gold `hate_snippet` spans;
`data/gt/HateMM/hate_spans.json`) — the same free-iteration calibration set as P10
(HateMM train hateful, n=266 both-class videos). **No HateClipSeg contact.**

**Three gold-span predictors, each → per-video within-video AUC** (map windows → 1-fps
seconds by the frozen `min(K-1,⌊mK/D⌋)` rule; per-video AUC over both-class videos;
`p11_probe_hatemm.py`, reusing the p10/p6 estimators bit-for-bit):

1. **MLLM (arm-B teacher):** 72B A-fuse per-segment density (existing asset
   `train_segscoreK30_p10-p6-72b-bnb4-fuse.jsonl`) → HateMM calib wv-AUC = **0.5913**
   (already in the P10-b leaderboard; re-verified by the probe). 7B K30 (0.5387) and
   72B K4 raw are reported for granularity context.
2. **MIL-proxy (arm-A analogue) — the side we must compute:** a **top-k MIL LINEAR
   head** on HateMM K=4 subclip CLIP features (existing cache
   `train_subclipK4_*.pt`, the only HateMM window features on disk), trained on the
   744 train videos' **video labels**, **5-fold cross-validated by video** (held-out
   segment logits, no leakage), per-segment logit → wv-AUC on the 266 calibration
   hateful videos. This is the cheap linear-head preview of arm A. (Granularity note:
   MIL features are K=4; densifying to K=30 *hurt* the visual-key localiser in
   `EVAL_localization_hateclipseg.md §4.4` (0.526→0.514), so K=4 is if anything the
   **stronger** MIL granularity → using it makes the gate **harder** to pass =
   conservative.)
3. **memory (arm-C analogue, context, NOT gating):** consensus-kNN vote of a
   cross-dataset memory (MHC-video, matching the P6 `mhc_video` swap-control) on the
   same HateMM K=4 windows → wv-AUC (expected ≈ 0.50–0.51, the "half-evidence"
   anchor).

**GATE (binding):** `wv-AUC(MLLM 72B A-fuse) − wv-AUC(MIL-proxy) ≥ +0.03` **AND** the
paired bootstrap 95% CI on the per-video Δ **excludes 0** (significant). Robustness
check (reported, directional): the granularity-matched `wv-AUC(MLLM 72B K4) −
wv-AUC(MIL-proxy K4)` is positive.

**KILL:** gate fails (MLLM advantage over the MIL proxy < +0.03 or not significant) ⇒
**P11 is killed here, no training job is submitted**, reported honestly as: the MLLM
weak label is not a better localization teacher than video-label MIL, so a distilled
head has no headroom.

## 5. Training cost (only if the probe passes)

Segment features are the existing K=30 CLIP cache; the head is a ~0.3M-param MLP →
each arm×seed is a **single short GPU job** (minutes; the whole 3-arm × 3-seed grid is
one small sbatch array, ≪ 1 GPU-hour). No new feature extraction is needed for the
HateClipSeg arms (subclipK30 cache + MLLM/memory score files all exist). If a
granularity-matched K=30 MIL-proxy on HateMM is later wanted for the probe, it needs
one short HateMM subclipK30 extraction job — **not** run unless the K=4 gate is
ambiguous.

### Decision chain (recorded verbatim, no rule-shopping in either direction)

1. The **committed letter gate passed** (+0.0386, CI excl 0). But its two sides differ
   in BOTH granularity (K30-fused vs K4) and operator (coarse×fine fusion vs raw), and
   the committed robustness check came back **+0.0058 ≈ 0** — exactly the "K=4 gate is
   ambiguous" condition §5 pre-authorized resolving with a K30 extraction.
2. The K30 remedy was executed (SLURM job **12616**, HateMM train subclipK30/M120,
   1h00, COMPLETED; reusable cache
   `data/CLIP_Embedding/HateMM/train_subclipK30_*.pt`; 744 videos, 1 zero-vector
   guard, consistent with prior runs). The **binding matched rule** — best-vs-best
   under the identical A-fuse rank-fusion operator, same "+0.03 AND CI excl 0" bar —
   was fixed in `p11_probe_hatemm.py` **before** the K30 cache landed.
3. **Matched verdict: FAIL.** Raw-vs-raw at both K (+0.006, +0.014, both n.s.) and
   fused-vs-fused (+0.0359, CI includes 0, sign-p 0.13) — the MLLM's weak-label
   advantage over a video-label MIL proxy is **not significant at any matched
   comparison**. Killing on the matched test despite the confounded letter-pass is
   the *conservative* direction — the opposite of bar-shopping.

### Why the kill is decisive for the §3 success line (not just the gate)

- **B − A ≥ +0.05 is unreachable:** the teacher's entire advantage over the MIL proxy
  is ≤ +0.036 (n.s. at n=266). A distilled student cannot exceed its teacher's signal
  advantage; demanding +0.05 significant on the *smaller* HateClipSeg test (119
  videos) from a ≤+0.036-n.s. teacher edge is hopeless.
- **B ≥ 0.65 is unreachable:** the teacher itself scores 0.5913 (HateMM) / 0.5755
  (HateClipSeg test, P10-b) — the ceiling distillation could transfer sits ~0.07–0.09
  below the absolute bar.
- **Mechanism (the campaign's recurring shape, now on the supervision axis):** the 72B
  A-fuse's zero-shot edge comes from the coarse×fine **aggregation trick**, not from
  being a better per-segment labeller — grant the same trick to a video-label MIL head
  and the gap collapses to n.s. A simple 5-fold linear top-k MIL head on frozen CLIP
  already reaches ~0.55 wv-AUC (matching 7B-class MLLM scorers), i.e. **video labels
  alone already contain most of what the MLLM weak labels would teach**. The MLLM
  density scores remain a *zero-training scorer* (P6/P10-b, the kept role); they are
  **not** a training signal that beats plain MIL supervision.

### VERDICT — **PROBE FAIL → P11 KILLED**

No training was submitted; the HateClipSeg test split (and its single touch) was never
consumed. Campaign accounting: P11 = the **13th pre-registered route**, killed
probe-side at zero training cost (one 1h feature-extraction job whose K30 HateMM CLIP
cache is a reusable asset). The MLLM's earned roles are unchanged — encoder + zero-shot
localization scorer (modest-plus) + guard-rail/audit — and the weak-supervision
*training* role is now also refuted. The declared P11 split (`p11_split.json`) remains
frozen and unconsumed should any differently-premised HateClipSeg training route ever
be proposed.
