# Annotation-escalation prediction / annotation as the VoI cost axis

**stage:** `archived`  ·  **outcome:** `negative`

## Thesis
Predict which items will consume a third annotator and route the annotation budget accordingly, using annotation rather than compute as the cost axis of value-of-information.

## Key risks
KILLED in cross-model triage (2026-08-09) as a direct re-skin of prohibited per-sample escalation/abstention routing (never-claim item 12), AND because observational escalation records cannot establish annotator-hours saved at fixed label quality - if a predicted item still needs the third vote, the policy changes ordering, not cost.
