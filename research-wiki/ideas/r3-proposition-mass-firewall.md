# Proposition-mass firewall

**stage:** `piloted`  ·  **outcome:** `negative`

## Thesis
OCR/ASR tokens are soft-assigned to proposition units and each proposition receives one conserved unit of evidence mass, doubly-stochastically allocated across its duplicates, boxes and channels, so formatting cannot multiply semantic mass.
