# Metric conventions for frame-level video localization / VAD — literature verification

Date: 2026-08-30. Verified via arXiv/CVF/AAAI sources (fetch-verified quotes marked "verbatim"; everything else paraphrased from the cited source).

## Q1. Pooled frame-level ROC-AUC ("micro AUC") — CONFIRMED standard

- **Sultani, Chen, Shah, "Real-World Anomaly Detection in Surveillance Videos", CVPR 2018, arXiv:1801.04264** (UCF-Crime). Verbatim: "Following previous works on anomaly detection, we use frame based receiver operating characteristic (ROC) curve and corresponding area under the curve (AUC) to evaluate the performance of our method." Frames of all test videos pooled into one ROC; also reports false-alarm rate (FAR) on normal videos (Table 4). This is the metric every UCF-Crime / ShanghaiTech(weak) WSVAD paper inherits (RTFM ICCV'21, MIST CVPR'21, UR-DMU AAAI'23, VadCLIP AAAI'24, LAVAD CVPR'24, ...).
- **Georgescu et al., TPAMI 2021 (arXiv:2008.12328)** explicitly note (verbatim): "many previous works do not mention if the frame-level AUC is computed by (i) concatenating all frames then computing the score (this is the micro-averaged AUC), or by (ii) computing the frame-level AUC for each video, then averaging the resulting scores (this is the macro-averaged AUC)." The de facto default in the literature is (i), micro/pooled.

## Q2. Per-video macro ROC-AUC — established minority convention (one identifiable lineage)

### 2a. The Georgescu "micro/macro AUC" lineage (per-video AUC averaged over videos)

1. **Georgescu, Ionescu, Khan, Popescu, Shah, "A Background-Agnostic Framework with Adversarial Training for Abnormal Event Detection in Video", IEEE TPAMI 2021, arXiv:2008.12328.** Origin of the terminology. Exact terms: **"micro-averaged AUC"** and **"macro-averaged AUC"** (definition quoted verbatim above, from Sec. IV-B). Reports BOTH on Avenue, ShanghaiTech, UCSD Ped2. Motivation given: disambiguating an underspecified prior protocol ("We therefore report both frame-level AUC measures").
2. **Acsintoae et al., "UBnormal: New Benchmark for Supervised Open-Set Video Anomaly Detection", CVPR 2022, arXiv:2111.08644.** Benchmark officially adopts four metrics: micro-averaged frame-level AUC, **macro-averaged frame-level AUC**, RBDC, TBDC — "we consider both micro and macro versions, following [Georgescu TPAMI 2021]" (verbatim). Official eval code (`lilygeorgescu/UBnormal/scripts/compute_auc_score.py`, fetch-verified): micro = `roc_auc_score` on concatenated arrays; macro = `np.array(roc_auc_videos).mean()`; **single-class (all-normal) videos are kept in the macro average via a hack** — pad each video's gt and scores with one 0 and one 1 (`roc_auc_score(np.concatenate(([0], gt, [1])), np.concatenate(([0], scores, [1])))`) so `roc_auc_score` does not error. I.e., canonical macro-AUC averages over ALL test videos, not positives only (on Avenue/Ped2 this is moot — every test video contains anomalous frames).
3. **Barbalau et al., "SSMTL++: Revisiting Self-Supervised Multi-Task Learning for Video Anomaly Detection", CVIU 2023, arXiv:2207.08003.** Reports micro- and macro-averaged frame-level AUC (surfaced in the same convention search; follows the TPAMI protocol).
4. **Reiss & Hoshen, "Attribute-based Representations for Accurate and Interpretable Video Anomaly Detection", arXiv:2212.00789.** Sec. 4.3 (fetch-verified): reports **both micro-averaged AUROC and macro-averaged AUROC** on Ped2/Avenue/ShanghaiTech ("(ii) computes the AUROC score individually for each video and then averages"); notes verbatim-adjacent: "Most existing studies report micro-averaged AUROC, while only a few report macro-averaged AUROC."
5. **Croitoru et al., "Lightning Fast Video Anomaly Detection via Multi-Scale Adversarial Distillation", arXiv:2211.15597** (journal version exists) — also defines/reports the micro vs macro frame-level AUC pair (surfaced in the convention search; same Ionescu-group lineage). Same for the follow-ups benchmarking on UBnormal.

### 2b. Distinct but adjacent: pooled AUC/AP restricted to the ABNORMAL-video subset (NOT per-video macro)

- **Zhou, Yu, Yang, "Dual Memory Units with Uncertainty Regulation for WS-VAD" (UR-DMU), AAAI 2023, arXiv:2302.05160** (AAAI proceedings pp. 3773–3774, read directly, verbatim): "False alarm rate (FAR) and anomaly subset with only abnormal data are also applied in ablation experiments (Wu and Liu 2021; Feng, Hong, and Zheng 2021)." and "The **AUC_sub** and **AP_sub** are separately the AUC score and AP score of the abnormal video subset, which only contains abnormal videos. They are used to confirm the anomaly detection performance for anomaly video."
- **BN-WVAD, "BatchNorm-based Weakly Supervised Video Anomaly Detection", arXiv:2311.15367** (fetch-verified, verbatim): "**AUC_abn** and **AP_abn** denote the AUC and AP scores calculated on the subset of abnormal videos, respectively."
- UR-DMU credits the practice to **Wu & Liu, IEEE TIP 2021** ("Learning Causal Temporal Relation and Feature Discrimination for Anomaly Detection") and **Feng, Hong, Zheng, "MIST", CVPR 2021, arXiv:2104.01633**.
- **Key distinction:** AUC_sub / AUC_abn is still a POOLED AUC over the concatenated frames of abnormal test videos — it removes normal-only videos from the pool but does NOT average per-video AUCs. It answers "can you rank frames within the anomalous population", which is close in spirit to a per-positive-video metric but algebraically different.

### 2c. Benchmark-critique precedents for per-video / per-abnormal-video averaging

- **Ramachandra & Jones, "Street Scene", WACV 2020**: criticizes frame-level AUC (cannot localize within a frame; insensitive to the amount of anomalous region) and proposes RBDC/TBDC — spatial region/track criteria, not per-video averaging; RBDC/TBDC saw limited adoption outside the single-scene (Ave/ShT/UBnormal) line.
- **Liu, Wu, Li, Yang, "Rethinking Metrics and Benchmarks of Video Anomaly Detection", arXiv:2505.19022 (2025)**: critiques pooled frame-level AUC/AP (annotation ambiguity, latency-insensitivity, scene overfitting); proposes AUC_avg/AP_avg (averaged over annotation rounds, not videos) and **LaAP**, whose latency-aware recall is computed video-level and **averaged across all abnormal videos** — a recent top-line precedent for per-abnormal-video averaging as a robustness axis.

## Q3. AP conventions — pooled frame AP confirmed; no per-video macro AP found

- **Wu et al., "Not only Look, but also Listen: Learning Multimodal Violence Detection under Weak Supervision", ECCV 2020, arXiv:2007.04687** (XD-Violence): metric is **frame-level average precision (AP)**. Official code (`Roc-Ng/XDVioDet/test.py`, fetch-verified): predictions of all test videos concatenated into one array against one pooled gt array, then a single `precision_recall_curve` + `auc(recall, precision)` (i.e., pooled frame-level PR-AUC/AP; `np.repeat(pred, 16)` maps snippet scores to frames). **Pooled, not per-video.** All XD-Violence followers (VadCLIP AAAI'24: 84.51% AP; LAVAD CVPR'24; BN-WVAD; UR-DMU: Table 2 "frame-level performance AP") use this pooled AP.
- Per-video/within-video **macro AP**: **no paper found** using macro-averaged frame AP for VAD localization. The only subset variant is the pooled AP_sub/AP_abn above.
- **Do not conflate with TAL mAP@tIoU** (THUMOS'14 / ActivityNet convention): segment-level — predicted segments matched to gt segments at temporal-IoU thresholds, AP per class, averaged. VadCLIP-style "fine-grained WSVAD" borrows segment mAP@tIoU; that is a different metric family from frame-level AP and should be cited as such if mentioned.

## Q4. Bottom line for the paper

- **{pooled frame ROC-AUC, pooled frame AP}** = unambiguously standard (Sultani CVPR'18 lineage; Wu ECCV'20 lineage). Safe to present as "the standard frame-level detection metrics".
- **Per-positive-video macro frame ROC-AUC** = an **established minority robustness axis, not a novel invention — but not standard verbatim either.** Two established components must be composed and stated explicitly:
  1. macro (per-video) averaging of frame AUC — Georgescu TPAMI'21 terminology, adopted as an official benchmark metric by UBnormal CVPR'22 and used by SSMTL++, Reiss & Hoshen, Croitoru et al.;
  2. restriction to abnormal/positive videos — MIST CVPR'21 / Wu & Liu TIP'21 / UR-DMU AAAI'23 (AUC_sub) / BN-WVAD (AUC_abn), though those pool frames within the subset rather than averaging per video.
  The exact composition "compute frame AUC per positive video, average over positive videos" matches neither lineage exactly (canonical macro includes normal videos via a padding hack; AUC_sub pools). **Recommended framing:** "pooled (micro) frame AUC and AP are the standard metrics [Sultani'18; Wu'20]; following the macro-averaged frame-level AUC of [Georgescu TPAMI'21; UBnormal CVPR'22] we additionally report the per-video AUC averaged over positive videos, restricting to positive videos as in the AUC_sub/AUC_abn protocol [UR-DMU AAAI'23; BN-WVAD] since AUC is undefined on single-class videos." One paragraph of definition + these citations fully covers a reviewer; no lengthy justification needed.
- **Five strongest precedents for the macro axis:**
  1. Georgescu et al., TPAMI 2021, arXiv:2008.12328 — "macro-averaged AUC" (defines the term, reports it on 3 datasets).
  2. Acsintoae et al., UBnormal, CVPR 2022, arXiv:2111.08644 — macro frame-level AUC as an official benchmark metric.
  3. Zhou, Yu, Yang, UR-DMU, AAAI 2023, arXiv:2302.05160 — "AUC_sub/AP_sub ... of the abnormal video subset" (positive-only restriction).
  4. Feng, Hong, Zheng, MIST, CVPR 2021, arXiv:2104.01633 (with Wu & Liu, TIP 2021) — origin of abnormal-subset evaluation, per UR-DMU's attribution.
  5. Liu et al., "Rethinking Metrics and Benchmarks of VAD", arXiv:2505.19022 — 2025 critique paper averaging its latency metric per abnormal video; plus Reiss & Hoshen, arXiv:2212.00789 (micro+macro AUROC tables) as a sixth.

### Caveats / unverified edges
- MIST's own text was not fetched to confirm its exact abnormal-subset wording (attribution taken verbatim from UR-DMU's AAAI camera-ready).
- Reiss & Hoshen and Croitoru et al. venue status not pinned (cited by arXiv id).
- The Georgescu CVPR 2021 paper (arXiv:2011.07491 v1) reports only a single AUC; the micro/macro pair is anchored in the TPAMI 2021 paper — cite TPAMI, not the CVPR'21 paper, for the macro definition.
- No VAD paper was found reporting per-video macro AP; if the paper reports macro AP as well, that column has no direct precedent and should be labeled as an extension of the macro-AUC protocol.
