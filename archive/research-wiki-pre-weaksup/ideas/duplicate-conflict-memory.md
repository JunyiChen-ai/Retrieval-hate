# Near-duplicate conflict memory / naturally occurring minimal pairs

**stage:** `archived`  ·  **outcome:** `negative`

## Thesis
Treat near-duplicate upload clusters as one memory voting unit and mine same-footage / opposite-label pairs (hateful original vs counter-speech or news repost) as free contrastive supervision.

## Key risks
KILLED by pilot P-B (2026-08-09): near-duplicates are strongly label-CONCORDANT - 5 conflicting pairs observed vs 24.1 expected under label permutation [15,30]; post-hoc forensic reduced the genuine count to 1. No free minimal-pair corpus exists. Duplication itself is negligible (49 verified pairs in 4.98M).
