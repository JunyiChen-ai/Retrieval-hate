# HVGuard: Utilizing Multimodal Large Language Models for Hateful Video Detection

## One-line thesis
HVGuard is the first reasoning-based hateful-video detection framework: it uses an MLLM (GPT-4o) with staged Chain-of-Thought prompts to generate a cross-modal rationale, then fuses that rationale with per-modality encoder embeddings (text/audio/frames) via a Mixture-of-Experts network to reach a final hate classification, beating all prior tools on English and Chinese datasets.
