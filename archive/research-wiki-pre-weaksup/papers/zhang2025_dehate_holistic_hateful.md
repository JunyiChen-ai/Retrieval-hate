# DeHate: A Holistic Hateful Video Dataset for Explicit and Implicit Hate Detection

## One-line thesis
DeHate is the largest hateful-video dataset to date (6,689 videos from TikTok and BitChute) that goes beyond binary hate labels by distinguishing explicit/implicit/non-hate, localizing hate segments in time, tagging which modalities carry the hate, and specifying the targeted social group, exposing how poorly current uni- and multi-modal models handle implicit context-dependent hate.
