## CANDIDATE 1 — CTF: Causal-prefix Temporal-development key (single supervised pool of the frame-group tensor)  **[LEAD by prior×cheapness]**

**(a) Mechanism / bandwidth / injection point.** The S2S frameset banks, per video, an **ordered set of T cumulative causal-prefix group summaries** `{g_1..g_T}` (T=4 for the 8-frame cache; `g_t` = the model's summary of the video *through* group t, verified cumulative by F35/F36). Every route so far either **pooled** these to one vector (the incumbent key ≈ `g_T`) or **set-matched** them (S2S, dead). CTF instead constructs **one** retrieval key by a **label-supervised pool** of `{g_1..g_T}` — the key stays a single 3584-d vector matched by the *unchanged* top-20 signed-cosine kNN vote, so it is **inside the pool regime, not the don't-pool regime**. Two realizations sharing one gate: **(1a, $0) the "narrative-arc" channel** — append the causally-well-defined long-range increment `Δ = g_T − g_1` (what the later video *added* over its opening) to the pooled key; **(1b, GPU) a supervised attention-pool** `k = Σ_t α_t·g_t` with a tiny learned query trained end-to-end with the existing triplet+BCE head over frozen `{g_t}`. Bandwidth: representation-level (D2, the only winning class), ≤ one extra 3584-d direction. Injection point: **retrieval-key construction**, upstream of the vote.

**(b) W2-A branch-conditioning.** **INDEPENDENT of W2-A** (uses the vision-only frameset, not the grounded key). *If W2-A SURVIVES:* CTF becomes a **composable orthogonal channel** — re-run the same gate on the *grounded* frameset (temporal development of the transcript-conditioned vision), and the binding baseline `Z_best` is *raised* to include the grounded key `grd` (CTF must beat `concat(img,text,grd)`). *If W2-A KILLS:* CTF is the **next representation-level probe in line** — its premise (supervised conversion of frameset headroom) is untouched by W2-A's failure, which is about a *different* key-construction operation (cross-modal grounding, not temporal pooling).

**(c) Non-isomorphism.**

The distinction is load-bearing: **F37's own oracle proved +0.09/+0.14 convertible headroom exists in the frameset**, and killed only the *unsupervised data-driven* operator (MeanMaxSim) as the converter. F30 tested summed per-step deltas as an unsupervised kernel input at K−1=3 (thin); CTF tests a single long-range increment (1a) or a supervised pool (1b) via a **conditional-info** operator, not a retrieval kernel.

## CANDIDATE 2 — GIR: Grounded-Incongruity Residual as an isolated channel  **[W2-A-conditional; $0; salvages the grounded extraction]**

**(a) Mechanism / bandwidth / injection point.** W2-A banks two vision-side pools of the *same* frozen forward: the **transcript-conditioned** key `grd` (transcript-first order) and the **ungrounded** vision pool `ungrd_vis` (video-first control). GIR isolates the **incongruity direction** `r = grd − ungrd_vis` — "how what-was-said bent the visual representation" — and tests it as a **low-dimensional appended channel** to the incumbent key, rather than as W2-A's *full* grounded key. The bet: even if the full `grd` doesn't beat `concat(img,text)` as a whole key (W2-A's raw-bar test), the *differential* may be a cleaner, lower-variance carrier of the visual↔speech (in)congruity that a supervised head can convert. Bandwidth: one 3584-d residual direction (or its top-PCA subspace); representation-level (D2); injection at key construction.

**(b) W2-A branch-conditioning (narrow live window).** *If W2-A SURVIVES (grd beats concat):* GIR is **DEAD-by-subsumption** — `r` is a linear function of `{grd, ungrd_vis}`, so it carries no info beyond the already-winning `grd`. *If W2-A's oracle kill-switch FIRES (grd fully redundant over concat on every dataset):* GIR is **DEAD** — no convertible incongruity info exists at all. *Live ONLY in the narrow "P3-pattern" cell:* W2-A **fails the raw kNN bar but the oracle ceiling survives** (headroom exists in the grounded forward, but the full-key operator can't convert it). There GIR asks whether *isolating the interaction term* is the missing operator. This is a genuinely different question from W2-A and salvages the ~2–3 GPU-h grounded extraction rather than wasting it.

**(c) Non-isomorphism.**
- **vs W2-A:** W2-A uses the **full** grounded key `grd`; GIR discards the shared component and uses **only** `grd − ungrd_vis`, the isolated interaction term — a different object with a different failure mode (W2-A can fail because `grd`'s shared vision mass dominates the cosine; GIR removes exactly that mass).
- **vs W2-K1 disagreement-as-signal (documented-killed, D1):** W2-K1 was a **1-bit decision-side** disagreement flag between two vote pathways. GIR carries congruity **in the representation geometry** as a 3584-d direction feeding the unchanged vote — this is precisely the "residue → pursue in the representation" disposition W2-K1's own kill recorded, realized as a differential key rather than a decision scalar.
- **vs C3-nontarget (DEAD_AT_FUSION):** C3 fused a **generated MLLM text channel**; GIR generates nothing and adds no channel — it re-pools the *difference of two pools of one native forward*. **F35 note:** GIR does not touch temporal/per-frame structure (it is a whole-video vision-span differential), so the cumulative-causal objection does not apply.

**(d) $0 G0-cond pre-check.** Features-only on banked `grd`, `ungrd_vis`, and pooled img/text/CLIP (Modal-CPU, zero test-touch): C3-template conditional-info of `r` (PCA-reduced) over `Z_best = concat(CLIP img+text, Qwen img+text, grd)` — note `grd` is **inside** the baseline here, so GIR must show the *residual* adds info **the full grounded key does not already carry** (the strictest possible test; guarantees non-isomorphism to W2-A is measured, not assumed). Label-oracle calibration arm mandatory.

**(e) Kill-switches + survival bar.** K-GIR-1 ($0): conditional info of `r` over `Z_best`-incl-`grd` < +0.040 or CI-lower ≤ 0 or ≤ perm-null max → DEAD. K-GIR-2: calibration accZA < 0.99 → MACHINERY_INVALID. Survival → paired LOO kNN `concat(key, r_pca)` vs `key` clears the S2S raw bar on HateMM before any GPU.

**(f) Honest prior: LOW.** Conditional on landing in W2-A's narrow raw-fail/oracle-survive cell (itself not the modal outcome), and a differential of two correlated pools is a thin carrier. Its value is **efficiency**: it is $0, it *only* runs in the one branch where it is non-vacuous, and it converts a possible W2-A near-miss into a second, sharper shot at the same banked grounded forward.

## CANDIDATE 3 — APX: Acoustic-prosody incongruity channel, HateMM-primary (openSMILE $0 gate FIRST, model-download behind it)  **[new signal source; independent of W2-A]**

**(a) Mechanism / bandwidth / injection point.** The pipeline is **frames + ASR-transcript-text only**; the **acoustic waveform** — aggressive prosody, shouting, hateful chants/music, laughter over a slur — is the one modality physically present in every raw video and **entirely absent** from the representation. Crucially the transcript captures **words**, prosody is **orthogonal** (the *how*, not the *what*). APX adds a frozen **prosodic embedding** per video as a representation-level retrieval channel, framed as the D7-defensible composite: **prosody↔transcript-sentiment incongruity carried in the retrieval key** (a menacing tone under benign words, or vice-versa), not plain audio fusion. Bandwidth: new high-bandwidth signal source (D2's *second* winning class per REFLECTION §3.2); injection at key construction. **Deliberately HateMM-primary** (per §0: representation levers work on HateMM; EN is data-limited).

## CANDIDATE 4 — AVC: Audio-Visual temporal-correspondence key (prosody×visual co-occurrence as geometry)  **[higher-novelty audio variant; needs audio + frameset; gated behind APX's $0 audio-gate]**

**(a) Mechanism / bandwidth / injection point.** Distinct from APX's channel-add: AVC builds a key from **whether aggressive acoustic moments co-occur with specific visual segments** — a per-segment audio-visual **correspondence signature** (segment-level prosody aligned to the banked frame groups `{g_t}`, summarized into one vector). Hate often *is* this binding — a slur delivered over a specific face, a chant synchronized to a symbol. This is the "incongruity/binding as representation geometry, done with a genuinely new modality" that the visual-only incongruity line (W2-K1) could never carry. Bandwidth: representation-level; injection at key construction; consumes banked framesets + new segment-audio.

**(b) W2-A branch-conditioning.** **INDEPENDENT of W2-A**; same promotion logic as APX (live if W2-A kills / new-signal frontier). Composable with a surviving W2-A (grounded × audio-visual binding) but that is a distant compound.

**(c) Non-isomorphism.**
- **vs APX:** APX = whole-video prosody as an *added channel*; AVC = *temporal binding structure* between prosody and visual segments as the key — different object, higher composite novelty.
- **vs S2S/don't-pool (closed F37):** AVC does **not** set-match frame groups; it **summarizes an audio-visual alignment into one pooled key** matched by unchanged kNN. The cross-modal correspondence is the new bits, not a retrieval-metric change.
- **vs W2-C order-kernel / F35:** AVC uses **co-occurrence** (which acoustic energy aligns to which cumulative visual prefix), **not frame-local order** — it reads *which segment* the aggression binds to, a causally-valid per-prefix quantity (F36 onset-invariance), not a permutation/DTW over frames. It must still show (in its own prereg) that the alignment is not absorbed by an order-shuffle null (F30 discipline).

**(d) G0-cond pre-check.** **Gated behind APX's $0 eGeMAPS audio-gate** (no point extracting segment-audio if whole-video prosody is already redundant). If APX survives: extract segment-level eGeMAPS aligned to the ASR K-segment boundaries (already banked: `data/ASR/<ds>/*_asrK4_whisper-large-v3.jsonl`), build the correspondence summary, and run the C3-template conditional-info over `Z_best` + a **shuffled-alignment null** (audio segments permuted against visual groups — isolates *binding* from marginal audio, the direct analog of the F30 order-shuffle null).

**(e) Kill-switches + survival bar.** K-AVC-0: APX eGeMAPS gate fails → AVC never starts. K-AVC-1 ($0): conditional info of the correspondence summary over `Z_best` < +0.040, OR not above the shuffled-alignment null → the *binding* adds nothing beyond marginal audio → DEAD. K-AVC-2: calibration accZA < 0.99 → MACHINERY_INVALID. Survival bar: gate clears **AND** shuffle-null defeated **AND** raw S2S bar on HateMM.

**(f) Honest prior: LOW (lowest cheapness — most machinery, download-adjacent).** Highest composite-novelty story of the four (audio-visual binding as retrieval geometry is unclaimed in-domain), but it stacks every risk: needs audio extraction, an audio encoder (download ruling), segment alignment, and a shuffle-null it can fail; and the whole thing is dominated by APX's cheaper gate. Included as the honest high-ceiling long-shot, ranked last precisely because a rational prober runs APX's $0 gate first and only reaches AVC if it clears.
