## A. What was established today

**A1. The label-tuple bottleneck (F106/F107).** The deployed decision reads **only which items are
retrieved and in what order** — not their similarity values. Replacing every cosine with 1 reproduces
the deployed decision on **99.60 / 100.00 / 100.00 %** of train items (HateMM / MHC-ZH / MHC-EN) in the
raw arena. *Strength: strong, and it is a design fact, not a fitted claim.*
**Caveat you must carry:** the head-space leg (K-HC-3 = 1.0000) is **collapse-forced** — F107 §4.4 says
so itself — so it is **not independent corroboration**. One leg, not two.

**A2. The re-ranking / candidate-set family is closed by a coverage bound (F106).** A correct-label
neighbour is already inside the deployed top-20 for **99.33 / 100.00 / 99.82 %** of items, so expanding
the pool 20 → 400 buys a free-weight oracle of only **+0.0067 / +0.0000 / +0.0018**. *Strength: strong —
arithmetic over banked lists.*

**A3. The weighting family is capped by two theorems (F106).** Bailey & Jain (1978): for fixed `k`,
**uniform** weights minimise asymptotic error for all distributions. Samworth (2012, *Ann. Statist.*
40(5):2733–2763, arXiv:1101.5783): the optimal-weight regret ratio **decays with dimension**, and the
only analytic escape is **negative weights** — which the box bans. F105's λ-continuum is that theorem
measured at finite `n`. *Strength: strong on theory; Bailey–Jain's id is pre-arXiv and unverifiable,
though the claim is confirmed by multiple secondary sources.*

**A4. The metric-channel bound (F107) — ⚠ WEAKENED TODAY. Read A4 before citing F107.**
F107 ruled the metric channel closed partly because *"the objective is already at its optimum on its own
training signal, with ≤0.002 of headroom"*. **That step is RETRACTED** (see §B/F114): it used a **CLIP**
number; the deployed heads leave **0.0594 / 0.1085 / 0.1846** of train-side headroom — largest on MHC-EN,
where our arithmetic is tightest. **The conclusion survives but is now EMPIRICAL, not analytic**, resting
on (i) the isomorphism to F75/NCA, a *measured* GPU negative (0/8 formal, 7/8 KS-arm-dead), (ii) a weak
observational conversion bound (+0.0286 upper 95 % CI, but **R² = 0.027, MHC-ZH dev only**), and (iii)
F113's evidence that scores fitted on head keys memorise and generalise worse. **Do not cite F107 as a
theory-level door-closer.**

**A5. F113 — the raw pregate arena's error is ONE-SIDED: trustworthy for kills, not for leads.**
**9 of 9** raw positives fail to transfer to the deployed head space (median shrink **> 7×**, three
invert sign); **0 of 8** raw kills reverse. A screen that over-reports effect sizes cannot manufacture a
false kill, so **every raw-space closure is more secure than when it was written.** *Strength: the kill
side is 8 cells; see §B for what the positive side rests on.*

**A6. The mechanism for non-transfer (F113).** Raw and head top-20 neighbourhoods share only
**2.04/20** (HateMM) and **2.85/20** (MHC-ZH) items; **19.9 % / 12.7 %** of queries share **zero**. The
head also compresses the decision margin (median |vote| 0.51–0.66 → 0.95–0.99), collapsing the
re-weightable population from 43–46 items to 2–10. *Strength: strong, replicates on 2 datasets × 3 seeds.*
